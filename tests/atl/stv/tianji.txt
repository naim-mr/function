Tian Ji's Horse Racing -- original model (STV built-in benchmark)
================================================================

Reference
---------
Tool : STV (StraTegic Verifier), W. Jamroga, D. Kurpiewski, et al.
       https://github.com/blackbat13/stv   (newer: blackbat13/stv_v2)
Paper: "Fixpoint Approximation of Strategic Abilities under Imperfect
        Information", AAMAS 2017 / https://arxiv.org/pdf/1612.02684

Like "Castles", "TianJi" is a built-in STV model generator used as a
standard ATL_ir scalability benchmark (reachability of a win).

The story (classic Chinese strategy)
------------------------------------
General Tian Ji races his three horses against the King's three horses.
In each grade the King's horse is slightly faster than Tian Ji's, but
Tian Ji's horses are graded so that:

    Tian Ji top  >  King mid  >  King low
    Tian Ji mid  >  King low

Concrete speeds used in the C transcription:
    Tian Ji : low = 2, mid = 4, top = 6
    King    : low = 3, mid = 5, top = 7

Over three rounds each side races one not-yet-used horse per round.
Tian Ji's optimal strategy: sacrifice his slowest horse against the King's
fastest, then win the remaining two races -> majority (2 of 3).

Game structure
--------------
  - agents = { TianJi, King }
  - state  = which horses remain for each side  +  current score
  - each round both pick one remaining horse (a permutation over 3 rounds)
  - the faster horse wins the round.

Strategic property (the one encoded for FuncTion)
-------------------------------------------------
    << TianJi >>  F  ( wins >= 2 )        -- TRUE

NOTE on the C transcription (../tianji.c)
-----------------------------------------
Sets of "remaining horses" are encoded with per-horse availability flags
(no arrays). If a player selects an already-spent horse it forfeits that
round (value 0). This keeps the model numeric while preserving the
"each horse used at most once" constraint and the majority-win property.

Parameterisation for a benchmark family
---------------------------------------
Increase the number of horses per side (and rounds) to obtain larger
TianJi_k instances.
