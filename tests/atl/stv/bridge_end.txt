Bridge End-play -- original model (STV built-in benchmark)
=========================================================

Reference
---------
Tool : STV (StraTegic Verifier), W. Jamroga, D. Kurpiewski, et al.
       https://github.com/blackbat13/stv   (built-in "Bridge" / "Bridge End")

Informal model
--------------
An end-game of contract bridge: a fixed number of tricks remain. The
declarer plays against the defenders. Each trick, players play one card
from their hand (cards used at most once); the higher card of the led suit
wins the trick. Crucially this is an IMPERFECT-INFORMATION game: a player
does not see the opponents' hands, only the cards already played.

State: the remaining cards of each player and the number of tricks won.

Strategic property
-----------------
Can the declarer guarantee winning at least k of the remaining tricks?

    << declarer >>  F  ( tricks_won >= k )

C transcription: ../stv/bridge_end.c
  NUMERIC SIMPLIFICATION: 3 tricks, declarer hand {2,4,9}, defender {3,5,8};
  cards tracked with availability flags; a player reusing a spent card
  forfeits the trick. The hidden-hand / imperfect-information aspect of the
  real model is NOT captured by the numeric encoding.

Parameterisation for a benchmark family
---------------------------------------
Increase the number of tricks / cards per hand.
