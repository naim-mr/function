Futures Market Investor -- original model (PRISM-games case study)
================================================================

Reference
---------
Tool : PRISM-games (stochastic multi-player game, rPATL).
Case study page: https://www.prismmodelchecker.org/games/casestudies.php
Model: futures market investor (McIver & Morgan).

Informal model
--------------
An investor trades a stock against a market. Over successive months the market
value fluctuates (probabilistically) and the investor decides when to reserve
a position so that subsequent price changes accrue to its profit; a "bar"
phase models market conditions in which reservations are restricted. The
original rPATL analysis computes the maximal expected / guaranteed profit the
investor can secure against the market.

De-probabilised version for FuncTion
------------------------------------
Keep the controllability split (investor = coalition, market = adversary) and
ask sure reachability of the MAXIMAL profit:

    << investor >>  F  ( gain >= cap )

C transcription: ../game_theory/future_mi.c
  (the market picks the price ceiling cap (input("stock"), not fixed), moves the
   stock value within [0, cap] each month, and may bar reservation only an
   adversary-chosen FINITE number of months (bars = input("market"), not fixed
   in advance -- see BOUNDED_ADVERSARY.md) -- otherwise it could block the
   investor forever and trivialise the game. The investor chooses WHEN to
   reserve -- locking an entry price -- and WHEN to cash in, realising
   gain = value - entry. Since gain is bounded by cap, the target gain >= cap
   demands the PERFECT spread: enter at 0, cash at cap. No magic constant
   remains -- cap itself is market-chosen.)

MODELLING SCOPE: this is a SINGLE-TRADE model -- exactly one reservation
followed by one cash-in, after which the game ends. The `bars` budget therefore
applies ONLY before the reservation (the bar branch is unreachable once the
position is reserved); it merely guarantees the investor can eventually enter
the game. A multi-trade variant (after each cash-in: reset the position,
refresh the bar budget, and accumulate the gain across trades) is possible but
NOT modelled here -- and it would not change the UNKNOWN verdict, since a
worst-case market can keep every individual trade unprofitable.

Expected result: UNKNOWN (soundness witness). The investor has a genuine
two-sided timing lever (reserve + cash in), but against a worst-case
adversarial market -- which controls every value move -- it cannot SURELY
force a favourable spread; the quantitative original yields a probability < 1.
So  << investor >> F (gain >= cap)  is expected UNKNOWN, a soundness companion
to the TRUE strategic-safety benchmarks (cf. train_gate single-train). The
target is exactly the ceiling cap: reachable in principle (the cooperative
optimum is cap), so the verdict is "not guaranteeable" rather than
"structurally impossible".

Why this modelling corresponds
------------------------------
  - controllability split: the investor is the coalition; the market moves are
    the uncontrolled adversary;
  - objective: reachability of a profit threshold;
  - probabilistic market -> demonic non-determinism (worst-case price path).
    So  << investor >> F profit  is the qualitative (sure) core of the rPATL
    expected/guaranteed-profit objective; here it exposes the gap between
    "almost-sure under a real market" and "sure under a demonic market".
Preserved: investor-vs-market structure + the profit objective.
Abstracted: the price-move probabilities and the expected-profit value.
